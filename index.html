Our game

Our first game is out now on Google Play.

Card Swap

A card puzzle with 16 funny cartoon cards. Swap cards next to each other and line up each suit in the order A, J, Q, K. Beat the swap limit, or beat the computer.

Get it on Google Play

How to play

Easy to learn, hard to master. Watch a quick round:

1

Tap a card

Then tap a card next to it. The two cards swap places.

2

Line up a suit

Put the A, J, Q and K of one suit in order, in a row or in a column.

3

Watch your swaps

Your swaps are limited, so plan every move.

Easy

Play on your own with a swap limit.

Medium

Pick a suit and play against the computer. Nobody can touch the other suit's cards. Diagonal swaps are allowed. 15 swaps each.

Hard

Same duel, but only up, down, left and right swaps and 12 swaps each. If nobody finishes, it is a draw.
