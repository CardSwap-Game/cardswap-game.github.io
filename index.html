Card Swap

A card puzzle with 16 funny cartoon cards. Swap cards next to each other and line up each suit in the order A, J, Q, K. Beat the swap limit, or beat the computer.

How to play

Easy to learn, hard to master.

1

Pick a card

Tap a card, then tap a card next to it to swap them.

2

Line up a suit

Put the A, J, Q and K of one suit in order in a row or column.

3

Watch your swaps

Your swaps are limited, so plan each move before you tap.

Easy

Play on your own.

Medium

Play against the computer, and block its suit.

Hard

A tougher board and fewer swaps.
